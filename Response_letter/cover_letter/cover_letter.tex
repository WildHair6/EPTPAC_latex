Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript entitled \textbf{``Exact Prescribed-Time
Prescribed-Accuracy Control for Spacecraft under Dual Reaction Wheel
Saturation''} for consideration in the \textit{Journal of Guidance,
Control, and Dynamics}.

Modern multi-spacecraft missions, including on-orbit servicing, autonomous
docking, and cooperative reconfiguration, demand strict timeline compliance.
\textbf{Attitude maneuvers must reach the target state at exactly the
prescribed terminal time}, as failure to do so disrupts mission sequencing and
leads to mission failure. Yet existing prescribed-time controllers are
conservative and cannot guarantee exact-time arrival. Additionally, \textbf{both torque and
angular-momentum saturation of the reaction wheels must be simultaneously
satisfied throughout the maneuver}. Angular-momentum saturation is largely neglected in the literature,
and the minimum feasible terminal time under dual saturation constraints
remains uncharacterized, leaving parameter selection arbitrary.

To address these gaps, this paper presents a two-loop solution for exact
prescribed-time prescribed-accuracy control under dual reaction-wheel
saturation. The outer-loop planner generates a reference that reaches
the target attitude exactly at the user-specified terminal time while satisfying
torque and momentum limits, and the inner-loop nonsingular controller guarantees
prescribed tracking accuracy under uncertainties and disturbances. A
constructive parameter-synthesis rule determines the minimum feasible terminal
time. Simulations verify exact-time convergence, prescribed-accuracy tracking,
and strict dual-saturation compliance.

We trust this work will be of interest to the \textit{JGCD} readership in
spacecraft attitude guidance and control. This manuscript is original, not
currently under review elsewhere, and has been approved by all co-authors,
with no conflicts of interest to declare. Should you have any questions,
please do not hesitate to contact the corresponding author at the e-mail
address above. We thank the editor for their time and consideration.
