Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript entitled "Exact Prescribed-Time
Prescribed-Accuracy Control for Spacecraft under Dual Reaction Wheel
Saturation" for consideration in the Journal of Guidance, Control, and
Dynamics.

Modern multi-spacecraft missions, including on-orbit servicing, autonomous
docking, and cooperative reconfiguration, demand strict timeline compliance.
Attitude maneuvers must reach the target state at exactly the prescribed
terminal time, as failure to do so disrupts mission sequencing and leads to
mission failure. Yet existing prescribed-time controllers are conservative
and cannot guarantee exact-time arrival. Additionally, both torque and
angular-momentum saturation of the reaction wheels must be simultaneously
satisfied throughout the maneuver. Angular-momentum saturation is largely
neglected in the literature, and the minimum feasible terminal time under
dual saturation constraints remains uncharacterized, leaving parameter
selection arbitrary.

To address these gaps, this paper presents a two-loop solution for exact
prescribed-time prescribed-accuracy control under dual reaction-wheel
saturation. The outer-loop planner generates a reference that reaches the
target attitude exactly at the user-specified terminal time while satisfying
torque and momentum limits, and the inner-loop nonsingular controller
guarantees prescribed tracking accuracy under uncertainties and disturbances.
A constructive parameter-synthesis rule determines the minimum feasible
terminal time. Simulations verify exact-time convergence, prescribed-accuracy
performance, and strict dual-saturation compliance.

We trust this work will be of interest to the JGCD readership in spacecraft
attitude guidance and control. This manuscript is original, not currently
