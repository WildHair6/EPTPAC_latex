I am writing to share with you our latest manuscript, which serves as a continuation of our previous collaborative work, "Attitude Tracking Control for Rigid-Flexible Coupled Spacecraft with Guaranteed Performance Bounds". 

In this new work, we focus on an exact prescribed-time prescribed-accuracy control(EPTPAC) framework for rigid spacecraft actuated by reaction wheels. We tried to build a two-loop architecture to alleviate the premature convergence issue often seen in existing prescribed-time controllers. Specifically, the framework attempts to explicitly handle the "dual saturation" problem -- accounting for not only the control torque limits but also the angular-momentum storage capacity of the wheels, which is a critical hardware constraint sometimes overlooked in current literature.
